# Source attribution

This development pack adapts records 635, 95 from the [SchemaBench Escape Translation](https://github.com/thunlp/SchemaReinforcementLearning) release. The official repository uses Apache-2.0; individual upstream schemas may have separate licenses and must be reviewed before redistribution. The source rows were downloaded from an unofficial [Hugging Face mirror](https://huggingface.co/datasets/kimddalko/SchemaBench). The paired tasks and mandatory token-path check are local adaptations; scores are not official SchemaBench scores.
